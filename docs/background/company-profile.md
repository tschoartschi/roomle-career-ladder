# Company Profile – Roomle GmbH

## Overview

- **Founded:** 2014 in Austria (Linz)
- **Location:** Tabakfabrik Linz (creative/tech hub)
- **Headcount:** ~30 employees
- **CEO:** Albert Ortig
- **Legal form:** GmbH (Austrian limited liability company)
- **Collective agreement:** Austrian IT services KV (UBIT/WKO)

## Integration into HOMAG Group

Roomle is part of the HOMAG Group. The corporate hierarchy is:

**Dürr AG → HOMAG Group → Roomle GmbH**

### HOMAG Group

- Global market leader in wood processing machines
- ~7,000 employees
- 14 production locations
- Active in 100+ countries
- ~30% global market share in woodworking machinery

### Roomle's Role within HOMAG

Roomle provides the visual configuration and digitalization technology layer. Specifically, the "Rubens" 3D configurator platform powers **HOMAG INTELLIGENCE (HI)** — HOMAG's cloud-based platform connecting sales, configuration, and production for furniture manufacturers.

## What This Means Structurally

Roomle is a small, highly specialized software company (~30 people) embedded within a large industrial group (~7,000 people). It operates as a tech/product unit focused on 3D product configuration, while HOMAG provides the manufacturing ecosystem, customer base, and go-to-market reach in the furniture/wood industry.

## Core Product

**Roomle Rubens** — the first "Open Full Logic 3D Configurator" capable of seamlessly modelling digital products with photographic realism, including all parameters and structural elements. Features include:

- Full-logic 3D product configuration
- Appless augmented reality (AR)
- 4K rendering
- Cloud-based, scalable architecture
